## What this network does

People staying at transitional shelters, including hotels such as The Kelly, The Andrews, The Travellers Hotel, and sites connected with Breaking Ground, deserve safety, accessibility, privacy, and a meaningful way to report harm. We collect first-person accounts with consent, connect people to independent support, and help communities ask public officials for answers.

## About this project

Family Media Network is a community information project. We distinguish verified records from personal reports, protect identifying details where possible, and never require a survivor to publish their story. We welcome corrections and updates from residents and service providers.

<p class="fmn-small"><strong>Privacy first:</strong> Do not send names, immigration documents, medical records, or details that could identify you through an unsecured form. Use a trusted advocate or attorney when sharing sensitive evidence.</p>
