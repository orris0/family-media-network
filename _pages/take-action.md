Accountability is stronger when residents control their own stories. Choose an action that does not increase your risk.

## A practical sequence

1. Make a safety plan with a trusted person or advocate.
2. Keep a private timeline and copies of responses.
3. Send a focused request to the responsible agency and your city council representative.
4. Ask for a written response, a case number, and an accessible way to receive updates.
5. Share patterns, not identifying details, with community organizations and journalists you trust.

## Ready-to-use language

Use the [city official email template](/know-your-rights/) to request an investigation, preservation of records, an accessibility response, and protection from retaliation. If you are seeking compensation, ask a legal services organization about deadlines, evidence, and whether a claim is possible before signing anything.

## What we need from officials

- A clear, accessible process for reporting assault, abuse, harassment, and unsafe conditions.
- Prompt independent investigation and preservation of relevant records.
- Disability accommodations and language access without retaliation.
- Safe relocation options when a resident reports danger.
- Public reporting that protects residents' identities and immigration privacy.
