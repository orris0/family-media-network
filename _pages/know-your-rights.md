Your rights do not disappear because you live in a shelter, are disabled, are undocumented, or are asking for help. Rules and remedies vary by situation. A lawyer or qualified advocate can help you apply them to your case.

## Start here

- **Safety:** You can ask staff to respond to threats, assault, harassment, or unsafe conditions. If there is immediate danger, call 911 when safe. Ask for the incident number and the name or title of the person who received your report.
- **Accessibility:** You can request reasonable accommodations related to a disability. Ask for the request and response in writing, and keep copies. An advocate can help if a request is denied or ignored.
- **Language access:** Ask for an interpreter or translated information if you need it. You do not have to rely on a child or another resident to interpret sensitive information.
- **Privacy and consent:** You can ask how your information will be used before sharing it. Do not sign a statement you do not understand. Request time to review documents and seek advice.
- **Immigration safety:** Immigration questions are high-stakes. Consult an immigration attorney or trusted organization before sharing documents or signing anything.
- **Retaliation:** Keep a dated record if you are threatened with discharge, relocation, loss of services, or other punishment after making a complaint.

## A simple record to keep

Write down the date, time, location, people involved, what you personally saw or heard, injuries or property damage, witnesses, who you notified, and what happened next. Save copies outside the shelter if that is safe. Do not record people or conversations where it could create additional danger or violate a law.

## Email template for city officials

Copy, adapt, and remove anything that could identify you without your consent.

```text
Subject: Request for investigation and safety response at [shelter/site]

Dear [official or agency],

I am a [resident / advocate / community member] writing about reported safety and accessibility concerns at [site], including [brief, factual description]. The concern(s) occurred on or around [date/time] and were reported to [role/agency] on [date], if applicable. The reference or incident number is [number].

I am asking your office to:
1. Confirm which agency is responsible for investigating this report;
2. Preserve relevant records, including incident reports, camera footage, and accommodation requests;
3. Provide a written safety and accessibility plan for affected residents; and
4. Tell me how residents can receive updates without retaliation or loss of services.

Please confirm receipt and provide a response by [date]. I can share more information through a secure channel. Please do not publish my name, immigration information, medical information, or identifying details without my explicit consent.

Sincerely,
[name or "A shelter resident / community member"]
[safe contact method, if any]
```

The template is not legal advice. Consider sending it to a council member, the relevant city agency, an ombuds office, and an independent legal or disability-rights advocate. Keep the sent message and any reply.
