Start with the safest next step for your situation. You do not need to tell your whole story at once.

## Immediate and citywide support

- **Immediate danger or medical emergency:** 911, if safe to call.
- **NYC shelter and city services:** 311. Ask for an interpreter, accessibility support, or a complaint reference number.
- **Domestic or sexual violence support:** NYC HOPE, 1-800-621-HOPE (4673), or text 639-9623. Ask about language access and safe contact options.
- **Legal help:** Call 311 and ask for civil legal services, or contact a trusted legal services organization before sharing sensitive documents.
- **Disability rights:** Ask 311 for disability services and an independent disability-rights advocate. Request reasonable accommodations in writing.

## When you contact an advocate

Tell them what you need first: safety planning, an accommodation, a complaint, immigration advice, medical care, emergency relocation, or compensation. Ask whether the conversation is confidential, who will see your information, and whether there is a cost. A nonprofit or attorney may be able to discuss compensation, damages, or a claim; this site cannot promise an outcome.

<div class="fmn-alert"><strong>Protect your privacy.</strong> Use a safe device and contact method when possible. Do not post your room number, shelter location, immigration status, medical records, or unredacted evidence publicly.</div>
