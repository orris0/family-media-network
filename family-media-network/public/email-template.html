City Officials Email Template

Use this template to document concerns, request inspection, and request accountability for shelter conditions and safety failures.

Subject: Urgent Request for Investigation and Protection of Residents at [Facility Name]

To: NYC Department of Homeless Services, Mayor's Office, City Council Members, Oversight Committees, and relevant agency leadership,

I am writing to request an immediate investigation and intervention regarding conditions at [Facility Name], which is operating as a shelter or transitional housing site in New York City. I am concerned about repeated reports of violence, unsafe conditions, retaliation, denial of accommodations, and failure to protect vulnerable residents, including people with disabilities and undocumented residents.

Residents have reported ongoing problems including:
- physical intimidation or assault;
- retaliation after reporting safety issues;
- unsafe conditions that place residents at risk;
- denial of reasonable accommodations for disability-related needs;
- language barriers and insufficient access to information or support;
- failure to log or respond to complaints appropriately.

These conditions are unacceptable in a city-funded shelter or safe-haven setting. Residents deserve safety, dignity, and access to basic protections. We are asking for an immediate inspection, a review of staffing and safety protocols, and a formal response to documented complaints.

I am asking the City to:
1. conduct an immediate, unannounced inspection of the site;
2. review incident logs, complaints, staffing records, and oversight reports;
3. provide language access and disability accommodation support;
4. ensure no retaliation against residents who report conditions;
5. provide a timeline and written response regarding next steps.

This issue requires urgent action. Please respond promptly and provide a formal update on the steps being taken to protect residents and hold responsible parties accountable.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
[Phone or email]
[Resident, advocate, family member, or legal support contact]
