📘 Know Your Rights: New York City Shelter Protections

Every resident in a New York City safe haven, transitional site, or traditional shelter has legal protections under the law. Language barriers or citizenship status do not waive these rights.

  • Right to Safety: Facilities must provide 24/7 basic security monitoring. Incidents of physical violence or harassment must be officially logged by staff.
  • Right to Non-Retaliation: It is unlawful for facility staff or providers (such as Breaking Ground or other contracted operators) to threaten your shelter placement, transfer you maliciously, or restrict your access because you filed a complaint.
  • Right to Translation Support: Under NYC Language Access rules, you have the right to request all critical rules, intake paperwork, and disciplinary notices in your preferred language.
  • Right to Safe Accommodations: If you have a diagnosed physical or psychological condition, you are legally entitled to Reasonable Accommodations (e.g., specific elevator access, lower bunks, or medical partitions).
