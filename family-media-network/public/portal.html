About Family Media Network

Mission: Family Media Network is an independent advocacy hub documenting abuse, retaliation, unsafe conditions, and systemic failures in NYC shelters and transitional housing. We support survivors with legal resources and tools to hold city officials accountable.

Audience: Survivors, disabled residents, undocumented residents, advocates, family members, legal teams, and elected officials.

Facilities Tracked: The Kelly, The Andrews, The Travellers Hotel, Breaking Ground, BRC 25th Street, and other NYC safe havens.
